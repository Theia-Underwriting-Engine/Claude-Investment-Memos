USD.AI (CHIP) | Preliminary Memo for Theia
1. Business Overview

USD.AI is an on-chain credit market that lends stablecoins against NVIDIA GPUs. Depositors mint USDai (backed by PYUSD and other stablecoins) and stake it as sUSDai, which funds senior loans to GPU neoclouds. Each loan sits in a bankruptcy-remote Delaware SPV at 70-80% LTV with ~3 months of debt-service reserve and insurance, and title to the hardware is tokenized under the CALIBER framework. GPU Finance Ltd, a CHIP Foundation subsidiary, is lender of record. USD.AI Inc. (formerly Permian Labs) originates and underwrites the loans.

The business model changed in Oct-26: from hold-to-maturity to originate, season and sell. Under V1, sUSDai funded each loan for its full 3-year life and the protocol earned the origination fee. Since GLX launched on 1-Oct-26, sUSDai works as a warehouse. Loans are funded, seasoned for a few months, then sold to institutional buyers and the cash relent; large loans now bring in co-lenders at origination (Silver Point and Victory Park on the $106M NexGen loan). GLX handled more than $200M in its first month. Growth is now limited by loan buyers rather than deposits, which USD.AI says hit the utilization cap against more than $25B of inbound demand. The origination economics moved off-chain with it, to USD.AI Inc. Next on the roadmap: asset-backed securities, then profit-share products (Conor Moore, 18-Nov-26).

Four loans ($387M) have been placed through GLX so far. USD.AI says it has originated $531M across 20 deals (12-Nov-26). Live today: 16 active loans ($177M) at an 11.5% weighted APR, plus a $128.9M GB200 loan for a listed GPU cloud in escrow, for $305M deployed against a $463M asset base.

The governance token, CHIP, has a narrow cash claim. The protocol keeps 10% of sUSDai yield, about $3.5M a year today. On 28-Oct-26 the Foundation said it would route 100% of that NIM, net of its own costs, to CHIP purchases "from today". No vote has been held and no purchase amounts have been published. Origination fees (2-3%) and 60% of gain on sale are earned off-chain by USD.AI Inc.; the other 40% of gain on sale funds a Yield Reserve that backs a 7% CHIP Hurdle Rate for sUSDai.

3. Bull & Bear Cases
Bull
1. The product works. Loans went from ~$1M to $305M in a year at an 11.5% weighted APR with no defaults reported. Borrowers include an investment-grade, listed GPU cloud.
2. The pipeline is larger than the book. $220.6M of executed term sheets at 11.5-15% and $526M committed; projected sUSDai APY is 11.6% once deployed.
3. GLX can scale origination without scaling deposits. Selling seasoned loans at par or better recycles sUSDai like a warehouse line, so origination volume can run several times AUM.
4. Value accrual is switching on. The NIM is pointed at CHIP and USD.AI Inc. has started discretionary buys ($1M on ~27-Oct). The USDai NIM is a large dormant lever at scale.
Bear
1. CHIP gets 10% of the yield. ~$3.5M of protocol revenue against a $287M FDV (81x, vs Maple 7.6x). The origination economics sit in a private company.
2. Deposits are leaving. sUSDai fell 21% in three weeks ($558M → $442M) and stablecoin reserves fell to $157M. 30-day redemptions are funded from a thin buffer.
3. A subsidy ends in January. PayPal's PYUSD incentive is 1.6pp of the 8.01% gross yield; realized APY (6.8%) is already below the 7% hurdle.
4. Concentrated, correlated collateral. One $128.9M loan is 42% of deployed capital; GPUs depreciate fast and together. The anchor facility's borrower (QumulusAI) flagged going-concern doubt.
4. Helmer's 7 Powers
Counter-PositioningModerate
Banks and private credit can lend against GPUs, but not with permissionless stablecoin deposits, tokenized title and on-chain secondary sales. Incumbents copying it would have to rebuild their funding stack. Silver Point and Victory Park co-lending on the NexGen Cloud loan shows they will partner rather than compete for now.
Cornered ResourceModerate
CALIBER (GPU title as ERC-721 under UCC Article 7) and the GPU underwriting data behind "Compute IQ" scores are proprietary, but legal structures can be replicated.
5. Porter's 5 Forces
Bargaining Power of Suppliers (depositors)High
Capital is the input, and depositors move for 50bps. $118M left on 1-4 Nov. sUSDai must out-yield T-bill products after the PYUSD subsidy ends.
RivalryHigh
GPU-backed lending is a crowded private-credit trade (Blackstone, Magnetar, Silver Point, bank warehouses). Spreads compress as capital floods AI infrastructure.
Bargaining Power of Buyers (borrowers)Moderate
Investment-grade borrowers negotiate down to 9% (the $128.9M loan); smaller neoclouds pay 12-15% but carry more risk.
Threat of SubstitutesModerate
For depositors: Maple, Ethena and T-bill tokens. For borrowers: vendor financing and leasing.
Threat of New EntrantsModerate
On-chain GPU credit needs legal structure, underwriting and deposits; several crypto credit protocols could add it.
6. What Drives the Token?
  • Proof that CHIP purchases are happening: the first published programmatic buy amount.
  • Deposit trend: sUSDai back above $500M, or a further run toward the 90% utilization cap.
  • Supply: the 16-Apr-27 cliff (~2.76B CHIP, more than today's float) will dominate price action as it approaches.
  • Credit events: any markdown, liquidation or below-par GLX liquidity sale.
7. What Drives the Business?

CHIP's revenue = sUSDai AUM × gross yield × 10% keep, minus Foundation costs, plus whatever USD.AI Inc. chooses to buy. AUM is capped by deposits, and yield = utilization × loan APR + reserve yield. USD.AI Inc.'s own revenue scales with originations × (2-3% fee + 60% of gain on sale), which is why the protocol favours velocity over balance-sheet growth. The model's key distinction: velocity enriches USD.AI Inc. equity; CHIP only sees it through discretionary buys.

12. Is This a Theia Investment?
Not yet. Underwrite again after the first published CHIP purchases and once deposits stabilize, with the Apr-27 cliff in view. USD.AI built a real GPU credit franchise in a year. After a 41% drawdown, the draft weights now show upside: $0.0473 a token, 1.6x MOIC, 18% IRR. But that is below the 40% hurdle, with P(loss) of 70%, and nearly all of the value sits in the two cases where originations scale and USD.AI Inc. shares its economics. CHIP's confirmed claim is a 10% slice of a shrinking sUSDai yield, and 2.76B tokens, more than the entire float, unlock in five months.

What would change the answer:

  • Published, recurring CHIP purchases from the NIM, with a burn or treasury policy.
  • sUSDai back above $600M after the PYUSD roll-off, with utilization below 85%.
  • USD.AI Inc. committing a fixed share of origination / gain-on-sale revenue to CHIP.
  • The escrowed $128.9M loan funding cleanly, and no QumulusAI impairment.
  • Price near the draft PV (~$0.017) ahead of the cliff.
