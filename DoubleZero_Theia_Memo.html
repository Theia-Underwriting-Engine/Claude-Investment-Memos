DoubleZero (2Z) | Preliminary Memo for Theia
1. Business Overview

DoubleZero is a private, dedicated fiber network for blockchains and trading firms, launched by Austin Federa (ex-Solana Foundation) and Andrew McConnell (Malbec Labs) with Jump Crypto as a core contributor. Fourteen contributors (Jump, DRW / Cumberland, Galaxy and others) lease links into the network. More than 60% of Solana stake now routes block propagation over DZ, where round-trip latency runs 21.6% below the public internet across 66 corridors.

The business has changed shape in a year. From Oct-25 to Apr-26 it charged validators 5% of block rewards and priority fees, earning $1.65M at up to $333k a month. It dropped that fee at epoch 939 to drive adoption and now monetizes through Edge, which sells low-latency market data by the seat. Edge carries Solana shreds (Apr-26), Kalshi (Aug-26), Hyperliquid (Sep-26), Phoenix perps (Sep-26) and Binance spot and futures (Oct-26). Edge for Builders (testnet, 27-Oct-26) lets third parties publish their own feeds.

The token, 2Z, has one economic right: 10% of network gross is burned. The other 90% goes to fiber contributors and validators / client teams. In Q2-26 Edge grossed ~$330k from 115 seats (~$1.5M ARR), so holders received ~$0.15M a year. On-chain Edge payments tracked by DefiLlama then fell from $133k in May to $0 in Oct-Nov. The Q3 token-holder update, which should explain this, has not been published.

3. Bull & Bear Cases
Bull
1. Distribution is already won. More than 60% of Solana stake runs on DZ, and Triton measured Edge shreds winning ~90% of leader slots. No rival relay has that footprint, and Jito shut ShredStream on 5-Sep-26 and pointed users to Edge.
2. Edge is becoming a multi-venue feed. Five venues in six months, with Hyperliquid's bundle listing at up to $9k a month. Edge for Builders could turn DZ into the network where venues publish, not just a relay.
3. The pool is real and growing. TradFi real-time data and connectivity is a ~$9bn business (CME data $803M, ICE data & connectivity $1.03bn in FY2025). The crypto slice is ~$270M growing to ~$652M by FY2029E on the house path, and DZ holds 0.6%.
4. The burn is a lever. A governance vote from 10% to 30% triples holder accrual with no new customers. In the base case that is $3.7M to $11.0M a year.
Bear
1. The token barely accrues. ~$0.15M a year reaches 2Z against a $176M FDV (~1,170x). Even on gross, 117x is above every data or DePIN comp.
2. Unlocks swamp the burn ~190x. ~135M 2Z (~$28M at today's price) unlocks every month to Oct-2029, and Jump owns 28%.
3. Revenue may have stalled. On-chain Edge payments went from $133k in May to $0 in Oct-Nov. Fiat billing or resellers could explain part of it, but there is no Q3 disclosure.
4. The best fee was given away. Validators paid up to $333k a month; Edge peaked at ~40% of that. Re-charging validators risks the stake share that is the moat.
4. Helmer's 7 Powers
Cornered ResourceModerate
Low-latency fiber between the right data centres is scarce. The contributors (Jump, DRW, Galaxy) are trading firms that lease capacity in exchange for 2Z. That aligns supply, but the same firms could build private links if economics shift.
Network EconomiesModerate
Each validator that joins makes DZ shreds earlier and more complete, which makes Edge more valuable to traders, which funds more links. The loop is real but has only been proven on Solana, and validators pay nothing to join, so the network effect does not yet monetize.
Scale EconomiesModerate
The fiber is a fixed cost shared across venues; each new feed (Hyperliquid, Binance) rides the same backbone at near-zero marginal cost. The edge only matters if volume arrives.
5. Porter's 5 Forces
Threat of SubstitutesHigh
Alpenglow's Votor went live on 21-Oct-26. Rotor, which replaces Turbine shred propagation, is deferred, but once live it could compress the shred-latency lead that Edge sells. Exchange-direct feeds (Binance, Hyperliquid nodes) and colocation are close substitutes for multi-venue data.
Bargaining Power of BuyersHigh
The buyers are ~100-1,000 sophisticated trading desks who benchmark every feed in microseconds and switch when a rival is faster or cheaper. The free promos and resellers that appeared in Q3 suggest pricing is already under pressure.
Bargaining Power of SuppliersHigh
The suppliers take 90% of gross. Fiber contributors and validators are the cost of revenue. Venues (Kalshi waived its publisher share for only one year; Binance data is used without a confirmed licence) can withhold or reprice their data.
RivalryModerate
Helius LaserStream, bloXroute, Triton and Pyth Pro compete for the same desks. Jito's exit from ShredStream reduced rivalry on Solana shreds, but Triton now resells DZ shreds, which blurs partner and rival.
Threat of New EntrantsModerate
Replicating 14 contributors' fiber and >60% validator adoption is hard, but large trading firms (Jump itself) can and do build private links for their own use.
6. What Drives the Token?

2Z trades on network narrative and supply, not on accrual. The market-implied weighting that reproduces $0.0176 at a 40% hurdle puts ~50% on the two cases where governance raises the burn to 20-30%. The price fell 58% across the 2-Oct cliff and kept falling as ~135M tokens unlock each month. The near-term drivers are:

  • Unlock absorption: next tranche 2-Dec-26; ~$2.4M a month of supply at today's price against ~$3.3M of daily volume.
  • The Q3 update: whether Edge revenue moved off-chain (card / ACH, resellers) or genuinely collapsed.
  • Burn / staking governance: any proposal above 10% or a staking launch reprices the token on accrual.
7. What Drives the Business?

Gross = crypto data pool × DZ share + other pools (re-monetized validator access, private multicast). Three variables decide it:

  • Paying seats × price per seat. 115 seats at ~$13k a year on average in Q2. Hyperliquid bundles at $66-108k a year show the ceiling for a high-value venue.
  • Venue count and licensing. Every venue added is near-zero marginal cost on the backbone, if the data can be republished legally.
  • Validator monetization. The access fee was a $3.5-3.9M annual run-rate at 5%. Restoring any version of it is the fastest path to gross.

12. Is This a Theia Investment?
Not at $0.0176. Watch for the Q3 update. DoubleZero has built real infrastructure: most of Solana, five data venues and a credible path to being crypto's low-latency network. But 2Z owns 10% of a gross that was ~$1.5M in Q2 and may be lower now. On draft weights the package is worth $0.0153 a token (0.9x MOIC, (5%) IRR, 85% P(loss)). Only the two cases where governance raises the burn clear the hurdle, and that is exactly what the market already prices (market-implied PV $0.0171 ≈ price). There is no edge versus consensus while supply unlocks at ~190x the burn.

What would change the answer (re-underwrite triggers):

  • A Q3/Q4 update showing Edge gross above ~$500k a quarter including fiat and reseller billing (vs ~$330k in Q2).
  • A governance proposal raising the burn above 10%, or 2Z staking that routes gross to holders.
  • A licensed Binance or CME data agreement, or a second L1 validator set paying for access.
  • Price below ~$0.006 (the draft PV) with the business intact.
